Invoice types
=============

### Regular
- Standalone invoice
- Max. amount: one

### Proforma
- Must be issued before the final invoice
- Max. amount: one

### Advance
- Must be issued before the final invoice
- Each can specify its own items, totals, payment settings or currency
- Max. amount: unlimited

### Final
- Requires proforma/advance invoice to be issued first
- Max. amount: one
